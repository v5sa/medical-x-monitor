# Supply Chain Intelligence

Monitor supply chain disruptions, raw material costs, logistics issues, and manufacturing trends.

## Focus Areas
- Raw material prices (polypropylene, stainless steel, packaging materials)
- Logistics disruptions (shipping, port congestion)
- Manufacturing capacity changes
- Quality issues in the supply chain
- Cold chain requirements (for IVD reagents)

## What to Log
- Raw material price spikes
- Shipping/logistics disruptions
- Factory closures or capacity changes
- Quality alerts from suppliers
- Cold chain incidents
